ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Department of Computer Applications

Empowering the Future with Innovation and Application

Welcome to Computer Applications

The Department of Computer Applications at Vignan focuses on developing theoretical knowledge and practical skills in programming, database management, software development and system design.

Students explore emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Mobile Application Development, Cybersecurity and Cloud Computing.

The department encourages hands-on learning through coding labs, projects, internships, workshops and industry interactions.

ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Eligibility Criteria

Review the general eligibility information for each program.

01

BCA

Candidates should have passed 10+2 (Intermediate or equivalent) with at least 50% marks, as indicated in the department's admission FAQ.

02

MCA

Candidates should hold a bachelor's degree. Admission is based on ICET rank, with Mathematics preferred as indicated in the department's admission FAQ.

03

Ph.D.

Candidates should meet the university's prescribed postgraduate qualification, entrance and research admission requirements. Refer to the official Ph.D. notification.

Note: Eligibility requirements may vary by admission year and program. Check the official admission notification before applying.
